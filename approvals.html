AI agents that ask before they act

Orchy AI is a command center for AI agents that asks before it sends, posts, spends, deletes, shares or changes your calendar. Routine work moves along on its own, and nothing goes out without your OK. That's human-in-the-loop approval built in.

Orchy AI runs on your own computer as a local dashboard. Your agents can work in Gmail, Slack, Notion, Stripe and 1000+ apps through One, and every action that reaches the outside world waits for you first.

How it works

  1. Tell Orchy what you need. Type it out, or say it out loud with optional voice. One conversation starts the work.
  2. The right squad gets to work. Orchy routes the task. Your agents work inside the right workspace, with the right account.
  3. Routine work moves along. Reading, sorting and drafting carry on without stopping you.
  4. Orchy asks before anything goes out. Sending, posting, spending, deleting, sharing and calendar changes pause for your OK.
  5. You keep the final say. Approve it, or hold it for now. Nothing goes out without your OK.

Example

Sample, as shown on imorchy.dev. You ask Orchy for an agent that triages your inbox and drafts replies to the urgent emails. The agent sorts the new mail and drafts the urgent replies. Then it stops and asks: "Ready to send." You review the drafts and approve, or hold them for now. Only the replies you approved go out. Nothing else does.

FAQ

Can Orchy AI send an email without my approval?

No. Orchy AI asks before it sends, posts, spends, deletes, shares or changes your calendar. Your agents can read, sort and draft, but a send waits for your OK. Nothing goes out without it, so a drafted reply stays a draft until you approve it in Orchy AI.

Which apps do Orchy AI's approvals cover?

Orchy AI's approvals cover actions, wherever they happen. Your agents connect to Gmail, Slack, Notion, Stripe and 1000+ apps through One. Whichever app an agent works in, sending, posting, spending, deleting, sharing and calendar changes wait for your OK before Orchy AI lets them go out.

Does Orchy AI ask about everything?

No. Routine work moves along on its own, so your agents keep working while you do something else. Orchy AI pauses only for the actions that reach the outside world: sending, posting, spending, deleting, sharing and changing your calendar. You let go of the busywork and keep the controls.

Where does Orchy AI run?

Orchy AI runs on your own computer as a local dashboard, and you can open it from browsers you have paired with it. Orchy sends nothing to its authors. Your approvals happen in your own command center, on your own machine.
