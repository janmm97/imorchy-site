Questions, answered

What is a human-in-the-loop AI agent?

An AI agent that pauses for a person's approval before high-impact actions. Orchy's agents ask before they send, post, pay, change a calendar, delete or share.

Does Orchy ever act without asking?

Reading, research and drafting run alone. Anything that leaves your workspace or can't be undone waits for your Approve, unless you choose Always allow for it.

What does Always allow do?

It lets one agent, or every agent in a workspace, do one kind of action without asking. You can switch it off again.

Do approvals work in every client workspace?

Yes. Every workspace follows the same ask-first rule.

Hand off the work. Approve what goes out.
