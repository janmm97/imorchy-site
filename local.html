A command center for AI agents on your own computer

Orchy AI is a local command center that brings your AI agents, apps and workspaces together on your own computer. It runs as a local dashboard you open from paired browsers, and Orchy sends nothing to its authors.

Your computer. Your command center. There's no place like localhost.

How it works

  1. A home on your machine. Orchy AI runs as a local dashboard on your own computer.
  2. Open it from paired browsers. You reach the dashboard from browsers you have paired with it.
  3. Bring your tools. Choose a brain for each workspace from 7 harnesses (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider or a custom CLI) and connect 1000+ apps through One.
  4. Tell Orchy what you need. Orchy routes the task to the right workspace, with the right account.
  5. Keep the controls. Sending, posting, spending, deleting, sharing and calendar changes wait for your OK.

FAQ

Is Orchy AI a local AI agent orchestrator?

Yes. Orchy AI is a local command center that brings your AI agents, apps and workspaces together on your own computer. It runs as a local dashboard, routes each task to the right workspace and agent, and asks before anything is sent, posted, spent, deleted, shared or changed on your calendar.

Does Orchy AI send my data to its makers?

No. Orchy sends nothing to its authors. Orchy AI runs as a local dashboard on your own computer, and you open it from browsers you have paired with it. Your agents then work with the apps you connect through One, using each workspace's own account.

Can I open Orchy AI from another device?

You can open Orchy AI's local dashboard from paired browsers. The dashboard itself lives on your own computer, and only browsers you have paired with it get access. From there you can start work, check on your agents and approve anything that needs your OK.

What can a local Orchy AI command center connect to?

Orchy AI works with 7 harnesses: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider and a custom CLI. Your agents connect to Gmail, Slack, Notion, Stripe and 1000+ apps through One. You choose what works for each workspace, and each client's workspace keeps its own logins.

How do I set up Orchy AI on my computer?

Orchy AI is in early access, and setup is done with the team on the access call. Join the early-access list and book 30 minutes. The team gets you access, connects your tools through One, and maps out the first agent worth building for your workspace.
