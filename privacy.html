Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what personal information Orchy collects through imorchy.dev and the early-access process, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your choices.

The short version:

  • The Orchy app runs on your own computer. It sends nothing to us.
  • Our website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or ads.
  • If you ask for early access, we keep the details you give us so we can contact you.

1. Who we are

Orchy is a product of One Systems, Inc., San Francisco, California. "Orchy", "we" and "us" mean One Systems, Inc. We are the controller of the personal information described here.

Contact: privacy@withone.ai. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

This policy covers imorchy.dev and Orchy's early access. One's other services at withone.ai have their own privacy policy.

2. The Orchy app: your data stays with you

The Orchy app runs locally on your computer. Your tasks, files, logs, keys and settings are stored on your computer. They are not sent to us. We don't collect telemetry or usage data from the app.

When you connect outside services, the app sends data directly from your computer to those services. They act under your own accounts and keys. Examples:

  • the AI engine you choose (such as Claude Code or Codex);
  • the routing provider, if you add a key (TypeSafe, OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway or OpenCode Zen);
  • One (withone.ai), for connected apps, under your own One account;
  • ElevenLabs and Deepgram, for voice;
  • Cloudflare, if you publish client pages.

Those services' own terms and privacy policies apply to that data. The Orchy app doesn't send it to us.

Because the app is local, you are responsible for your own copy of the data. That includes any personal data about your clients or contacts that you put into it.

4. Cookies

imorchy.dev sets no cookies of its own. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may set a strictly necessary security cookie, for example when it checks that a visitor is not a bot. That cookie is not used for tracking.

6. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers process data in the United States and other countries. When personal information from the EU, EEA or UK is transferred to the United States, we rely on the safeguards in each provider's data processing terms, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum).

7. How long we keep it

  • Technical data: kept by Cloudflare and jsDelivr for the periods in their own terms. We keep no website logs ourselves.
  • Early-access requests: until early access ends plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.
  • Booking details: until early access ends plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.
  • Emails: until your question or request is resolved plus 12 months, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access your personal information and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on consent.

To use a right, email privacy@withone.ai. We will confirm your identity by asking you to reply from the email address we have on file. We answer within one month (EU/UK) or 45 days (California), or tell you if we need more time as the law allows. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

EU and UK: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. California residents

In the past 12 months we collected these categories of personal information:

  • Identifiers (name, email, IP address), from you and your browser;
  • Internet or network activity (pages requested, browser data), from your browser;
  • Professional information (type of work or business), from you;
  • Audio or visual information, only if you agree to an access call being recorded.

We used them for the purposes in section 3 and disclosed them for those business purposes to the service providers in section 5.

We do not sell or share personal information. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of consumers under 16. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose that would give a right to limit. We do not use automated decision-making technology about you.

Your rights:

  • to know what we collected, from where, why, and who received it;
  • to delete it;
  • to correct it;
  • not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

Send requests to privacy@withone.ai. We verify them as described in section 8. An authorized agent may send a request for you with your signed permission; we may ask you to confirm your identity directly.

Opt-out preference signals (Global Privacy Control): we don't sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We still treat a GPC signal from your browser as a valid opt-out request for that browser.

10. Children

Orchy is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We protect the data we hold with access controls on our accounts and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method is perfectly secure. See our Security page.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use personal information you already gave us, we will tell you in advance by email or a notice on the site. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent first. We never apply such a change backwards without your consent.
