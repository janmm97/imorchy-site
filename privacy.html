What changed in Version 3.2

  • Early access has ended, so the waiting-list and early-access parts are removed or marked as past.
  • Payments now go through Polar, our payment provider. We still do not store card numbers (sections 3.2, 3.4 and 5).
  • Orchy now runs your agents' AI on its own OpenRouter account. OpenRouter is a sub-processor, and bring-your-own AI keys are no longer offered (sections 2, 3.3 and 5).

This policy explains what personal information One Systems, Inc. ("Orchy", "we", "us") collects through imorchy.dev, and hosted Orchy at app.imorchy.dev, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your choices.

The short version:

  • Orchy AI runs on our server in North America, so what you put in it is stored with us.
  • For hosted Orchy, you decide what goes in. We are your processor for that content and use it only to run your Orchy. We never use it to train AI models.
  • Our website sets no cookies of its own and shows no ads. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without following you across other sites. Hosted Orchy sets one sign-in cookie.

We are the controller of the data in sections 3.1 (website) and 3.2 (hosted account and sign-in). For the content you put into hosted Orchy (section 3.3), you are the controller and we are your processor; our Data Privacy page and DPA apply.

We have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK.

One and Orchy. One Systems, Inc. also runs One (withone.ai), the service Orchy uses to connect your apps. One is not an outside company: it is us. When you use One directly, with your own One account outside Orchy, One's own terms and privacy policy apply (privacy contact: privacy@withone.ai). In hosted Orchy, One Connect is part of our service and this policy and our DPA apply.

Read this with: our Terms and the Data Privacy page.

2. Hosted Orchy: what we store and where

Orchy runs on our server in North America, so we store what you put in it: your tasks, files, logs, settings, your agents' memory and chat history, and the data your agents bring in from the apps you connect. It sits in your own container on our server. Your account details (email, sign-in and a log of app-connection calls, without message contents) are kept by our control service on the same server. The server is at OVHcloud, with OVHcloud's daily automated backup. Our own encrypted backups are kept with Cloudflare, in Eastern North America.

You are the controller of that content and we are your processor (section 3.3). We use it only to run your Orchy, and we never use it to train or improve AI models.

Orchy runs your agents' AI on its own OpenRouter account, so your agents send prompts and files to OpenRouter. OpenRouter passes prompts to the model provider that runs the chosen model; each provider's own data terms apply. OpenRouter is one of our sub-processors (section 5). When you connect apps such as Gmail or Calendar, our own service One Connect handles the connection (section 1). TypeSafe, our routing provider, receives the text of each request (section 3.3).

The companies that help us run Orchy are in section 5. How long we keep each kind of data, and how deletion works, is in section 7.

3.3 What you put into hosted Orchy (you are the controller; we are your processor)

Tasks, files, logs, settings, client data and any personal data about your own contacts live in your own container on our server. We process this only to run your Orchy and on your instructions, as the DPA sets out. We do not look at it except as needed to keep the service running, fix problems you ask us to fix, or meet the law. We never use it to train or improve AI models. Your agents send prompts and files to OpenRouter, on Orchy's own account, and OpenRouter passes them to the model provider that runs the chosen model; their terms apply (section 5).

On hosted Orchy, the text of each request you give Orchy is sent to TypeSafe, our routing provider, on Orchy's own account, so it can pick how to handle the task. TypeSafe is one of our sub-processors (section 5).

3.4 What we don't do

  • We don't use cookies, advertising pixels or cross-site tracking on our public website. We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and gives us aggregate numbers only.
  • We don't use analytics cookies, tracking code or outside analytics companies in hosted Orchy. The product usage events in section 3.2 stay on our own server.
  • We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We don't use your data, including hosted content, to train AI models.
  • We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
  • Polar, our payment provider, takes payments and handles your card details. We never store your full card number.

Is it required? You choose whether to give us anything. Without an email address we can't create your account or contact you.

4. Cookies

  • imorchy.dev sets no cookies of its own. Cloudflare, our hosting and security provider, may set a strictly necessary security cookie, for example when it checks that a visitor is not a bot. It isn't used for tracking. Cloudflare Web Analytics on imorchy.dev sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • app.imorchy.dev sets __Host-orchy_cp, a strictly necessary sign-in cookie. It works only on app.imorchy.dev, lasts 30 days or until you sign out, is HttpOnly and Secure, and holds a random value (we store only its hash). When you open your Orchy, its own address gets a separate sign-in cookie, __Host-orchy_t, that works only there and ends when your app.imorchy.dev session ends. During Google sign-in we also set a short-lived orchy_cp_google cookie that we clear when sign-in finishes. We use neither for tracking or advertising.

6. Where your data is, and international transfers

Hosted Orchy runs on a server in North America. Encrypted backups are stored with Cloudflare in Eastern North America. We are a US company, and several providers are in the US or run global networks. When personal data of people in the EU, UK or Switzerland is sent to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards (GDPR Art. 46). For providers in the US, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) where the provider is certified, or on standard contractual clauses in the provider's data processing terms.

7. How long we keep it

  • Technical data: kept by Cloudflare and jsDelivr for the periods in their own terms. We keep no website logs ourselves.
  • Old access-request answers (from the earlier form): deleted 90 days after we approved or turned you down, or sooner if you ask.
  • Support emails: kept for 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Account record (your email and your Terms acceptance): while your account exists and for 30 days after you delete it. Then we replace your email and clear the personal details in the record.
  • Hosted content: Your files, published copies and email are deleted within 30 days of you clicking "Delete my Orchy" or of your access being removed. Your final encrypted backup is removed 14 days after it was made, as every backup is. OVHcloud's server backups are replaced within 7 days.
  • Product usage events: 13 months, then deleted. They are deleted sooner, within 30 days, when you click "Delete my Orchy" or your access is removed.
  • Sign-in cookie and session: 30 days at most, or until you sign out.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal information and get a copy, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Email support@imorchy.dev. We confirm your identity by asking you to reply from the email address on file. We answer within one month (EU/UK) or 45 days (California), or tell you if we need more time as the law allows. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. If you ask about content in a hosted Orchy that belongs to a business customer, we send your request to that customer, who is the controller.

EU and UK: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

In the past 12 months we collected these categories:

  • Internet or network activity (pages requested, browser data), from your browser, and product usage events in hosted Orchy (section 3.2), from your use of it;

We used them for the purposes in section 3 and disclosed them for those business purposes to the service providers in section 5. For hosted content, we act as a service provider (processor) to you.

We do not sell or share personal information. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing data of consumers under 16. We don't use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose that would give a right to limit. We don't use automated decision-making technology about you.

Your rights: to know what we collected, from where, why and who received it; to delete it; to correct it; and not to be discriminated against for using them. Send requests to support@imorchy.dev, verified as in section 8. An authorized agent may send a request for you with your signed permission.

Opt-out preference signals (Global Privacy Control): we don't sell or share, so there is nothing to opt out of. We still treat a GPC signal from your browser as a valid opt-out request for that browser.

10. Children

You must be at least 13 to use Orchy, and if you are under 18 you may use it only with the agreement of a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it. In some countries the age at which you can agree to online services alone is higher (up to 16 in the EU, under GDPR Art. 8), and there we require a parent's or guardian's agreement up to that age. Each app you connect has its own age rule; you must follow it.

11. Security

We protect the data we hold with access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS), web traffic reaching our server only through a secure tunnel, key-only administrative access, per-customer containers, AI keys kept only on our server, and encrypted backups. No method is perfectly secure. See our Security page.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use personal information you already gave us, we will tell you in advance by email or a notice on the site, and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. We never apply such a change backwards without your consent.
