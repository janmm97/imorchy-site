Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Version 2.0

This policy explains what personal information One Systems, Inc. ("Orchy", "we", "us") collects through imorchy.dev, the early-access process and hosted Orchy at app.imorchy.dev, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your choices.

The short version:

  • Two ways to run Orchy. The desktop app runs on your own computer and sends nothing to us. Hosted Orchy runs on our server, so what you put in it is stored with us.
  • For hosted Orchy, you decide what goes in. We are your processor for that content and use it only to run your Orchy. We never use it to train AI models.
  • Our website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or ads. Hosted Orchy sets one sign-in cookie.

1. Who we are

We are One Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 650 California St, Floor 7, San Francisco, CA 94108, United States. Privacy contact: support@imorchy.dev. We have not appointed a data protection officer.

We are the controller of the data in sections 3.1 (website, early access) and 3.2 (hosted account and sign-in). For the content you put into hosted Orchy (section 3.3), you are the controller and we are your processor; our Data Privacy page and DPA apply.

We have not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK.

One and Orchy. One Systems, Inc. also runs One (withone.ai), the service Orchy uses to connect your apps. One is not an outside company: it is us. When you use One directly (for example in the desktop app with your own One account), One's own terms and privacy policy apply (privacy contact: privacy@withone.ai). In hosted Orchy, One Connect is part of our service and this policy and our DPA apply.

Read this with: our Terms and the Data Privacy page.

2. The desktop app: your data stays with you

The Orchy desktop app runs locally on your computer. Your tasks, files, logs, keys and settings stay on your computer. They are not sent to us. We don't collect telemetry or usage data from the app.

When you connect outside services, the app sends data directly from your computer to those services, under your own accounts and keys. Examples:

  • the AI engine you choose (such as Claude Code or Codex);
  • the routing provider, if you add a key (TypeSafe, OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway or OpenCode Zen);
  • One (withone.ai), for connected apps, under your own One account;
  • ElevenLabs and Deepgram, for voice;
  • Cloudflare, if you publish client pages.

Those services' own terms and privacy policies apply, and they don't process that data for Orchy. The Orchy app itself sends nothing to us. One is run by our company (see section 1), so what you send through your One account is handled by One under One's own privacy policy. Because the app is local, you are responsible for your own copy, including any personal data about your clients or contacts that you put into it.

3.3 What you put into hosted Orchy (you are the controller; we are your processor)

Tasks, files, logs, settings, client data and any personal data about your own contacts live in your own container on our server. We process this only to run your Orchy and on your instructions, as the DPA sets out. We do not look at it except as needed to keep the service running, fix problems you ask us to fix, or meet the law. We never use it to train or improve AI models. If you connect an AI provider with your own key, your agents send prompts and files to that provider, and the provider's terms apply (section 5).

If you give us your own AI-provider key, we store it sealed (encrypted) and use it only to make your agents' calls. On hosted Orchy, the text of each request you give Orchy is sent to TypeSafe, our routing provider, on Orchy's own account, so it can pick how to handle the task. TypeSafe is one of our sub-processors (section 5).

3.4 What we don't do

  • We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or tracking on our public website.
  • We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We don't use your data, including hosted content, to train AI models.
  • We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.
  • We take no payments during early access, so we hold no card data.

Is it required? You choose whether to give us anything. Without an email address we can't create your account or contact you.

4. Cookies

  • imorchy.dev sets no cookies of its own. Cloudflare, our hosting and security provider, may set a strictly necessary security cookie, for example when it checks that a visitor is not a bot. It isn't used for tracking.
  • app.imorchy.dev sets __Host-orchy_cp, a strictly necessary sign-in cookie. It works only on app.imorchy.dev, lasts 30 days or until you sign out, is HttpOnly and Secure, and holds a random value (we store only its hash). When you open your Orchy, its own address gets a separate sign-in cookie, __Host-orchy_t, that works only there and ends when your app.imorchy.dev session ends. During Google sign-in we also set a short-lived orchy_cp_google cookie that we clear when sign-in finishes. We use neither for tracking or advertising.

6. Where your data is, and international transfers

Hosted Orchy runs on a server in Canada (Beauharnois). Encrypted backups are stored with Cloudflare in Eastern North America. We are a US company, and several providers are in the US or run global networks. When personal data of people in the EU, UK or Switzerland is sent to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards (GDPR Art. 46). The European Commission recognises Canada as giving adequate protection for commercial organisations. For providers in the US, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) where the provider is certified, or on standard contractual clauses in the provider's data processing terms.

7. How long we keep it

  • Technical data: kept by Cloudflare and jsDelivr for the periods in their own terms. We keep no website logs ourselves.
  • Waiting-list and early-access answers (including old form answers): deleted 90 days after we approve or turn you down, or sooner if you ask. While we are still reviewing your request, we keep them until we decide.
  • Access-call notes: deleted 12 months after the call, or sooner if you ask.
  • Booking details and support emails: kept for 24 months after our last contact with you, then deleted.
  • Account record (your email and your Terms acceptance): while your account exists and for 30 days after you delete it. Then we replace your email and clear the personal details in the record.
  • Hosted content: Your files, published copies and email are deleted within 30 days of you clicking "Delete my Orchy" or of your access being removed. Your final encrypted backup is removed 14 days after it was made, as every backup is.
  • Sign-in cookie and session: 30 days at most, or until you sign out.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal information and get a copy, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Email support@imorchy.dev. We confirm your identity by asking you to reply from the email address on file. We answer within one month (EU/UK) or 45 days (California), or tell you if we need more time as the law allows. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. If you ask about content in a hosted Orchy that belongs to a business customer, we send your request to that customer, who is the controller.

EU and UK: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

You must be at least 13 to use Orchy, and if you are under 18 you may use it only with the agreement of a parent or guardian. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it. In some countries the age at which you can agree to online services alone is higher (up to 16 in the EU, under GDPR Art. 8), and there we require a parent's or guardian's agreement up to that age. Each AI provider you connect has its own age rule, and some (for example Claude) require you to be 18 or over; you must follow them.

11. Security

We protect the data we hold with access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS), web traffic reaching our server only through a secure tunnel, key-only administrative access, per-customer containers, sealed AI-provider keys, and encrypted backups. No method is perfectly secure. See our Security page.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use personal information you already gave us, we will tell you in advance by email or a notice on the site, and ask for your consent first where the law requires it. We never apply such a change backwards without your consent.
