AI agents for finance admin

01

What an AI agent does in finance admin

Finance admin is the paperwork around money: invoices, reminders, summaries and month-end reports. An agent drafts them from the data you connect. When the next step is to send, pay, change or delete, it stops and asks you.

Orchy's agents don't give financial, tax or accounting advice, and they don't replace an accountant or bookkeeper.

02

Invoices and reminders

  • Invoice drafts from the details you give or your sheet
  • Payment reminder emails drafted in Gmail, in your tone
  • Reminders through Stripe, where your account is connected

Each one is sent only after you approve it. Paying anything always asks.

03

Spend summaries

Point an agent at a Google Sheet or your Stripe account and ask for a summary: what came in, what went out and what looks unusual to check. A person reviews the numbers before they are used.

04

Month-end report drafts

The agent assembles a draft month-end report from the sheets and summaries you connect, for you or your accountant to check and finish. Sharing it waits for your approval.

05

Your apps, connected through One

Connect Stripe, Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion and GitHub, plus 1,000+ more apps through One. Give each agent read or full access to only the apps it needs. A read-only agent can summarise without being able to change anything.

Example tasks

Type any of these in plain words. These are example prompts, not results. Check every figure before you use it.

  • Draft a reminder for each invoice that is more than two weeks overdue.
  • Summarise last month's Stripe payments in a short note.

Questions

FAQ

What is an AI agent for finance?

It is an AI agent that prepares finance admin in your own apps: invoice and reminder drafts, spend summaries and report drafts. In Orchy it asks before it sends, pays, changes or deletes.

Can an AI agent send invoices?

It can draft the invoice. It is sent when you approve it. Orchy asks before it sends by default.

Can an AI agent pay bills for me?

Orchy's agents ask before they pay, by default. You see the payment request first and approve or deny it.

Does Orchy do my bookkeeping or give financial advice?

No. Orchy prepares drafts and summaries from the data you connect. It doesn't keep your books, give tax, accounting or financial advice, or replace an accountant. A person reviews every figure.
