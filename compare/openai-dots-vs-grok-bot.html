OpenAI Dots vs Grok Bot compared

OpenAI Dots lives inside ChatGPT and adds rules for what it may do without asking. Grok Bot lives in Slack, Microsoft Teams and its own desktop and iOS apps, and publishes plan prices. Orchy is a third option, for teams that want agents in their own apps with approvals by default.

Last checked: 2026-11-24. Sources are listed at the end.

What each product is

OpenAI Dots. OpenAI describes dots as agents in ChatGPT. A dot has its own cloud computer and uses your connected tools. Source: chatgpt.com/features/dots/.

Grok Bot. Its page describes "AI teammates you can give real work to". Bots sign in to your tools and come back with finished work. Source: x.ai/bot.

Where they work

  • Dots: ChatGPT on web, mobile and desktop. You can connect a dot to Slack from the ChatGPT desktop app, and to a personal email. Texting is in limited testing for some Pro users in the US, Canada and Mexico. Access to your own computer is optional and starts off.
  • Grok Bot: Slack, Microsoft Teams, and the Grok Bot app on desktop and iOS. Bots can attend Google Meet, Zoom or Teams calls.

Neither page lists every supported app.

How each handles approvals

  • Dots: Custom Rules let you allow actions, require approval or block them. Auto-review checks actions that could affect your accounts or share information.
  • Grok Bot: Bots "check in when your approval is needed", and sensitive actions can go through Auto Review before they run. The page doesn't explain how Auto Review works.

Both give you control. Neither page states a default for every action, so check the settings before you give a bot access.

Availability

  • Dots: rolling out gradually, including to Plus and Business Premium in supported regions. For Plus and Pro, dots are not currently available in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland. Check OpenAI's help article for your plan.
  • Grok Bot: the page doesn't state regional availability. It offers "Get started for free", and the bot is included with eligible Premium, Super, Ultra, Teams and Enterprise plans. Teams is described as generally available.

Who should pick which

Pick OpenAI Dots if your team already works in ChatGPT, you are in a supported region, and you want detailed rules for what a dot may do without asking.

Pick Grok Bot if your team lives in Slack or Microsoft Teams, you want bots that can attend calls, and you want plan prices you can see before you talk to sales.

Consider something else if you want agents that work in your own apps with approvals by default, or dots aren't offered where you are. That is where Orchy fits.

Orchy, a third option

Orchy is a hosted AI workspace where a team of AI agents works in your connected apps. Agents research, read and draft on their own, and ask before they send, post, pay, change a calendar, delete or share. You build new agents in plain words with the Agent Builder. FAQ

What is OpenAI Dots?

OpenAI describes dots as agents in ChatGPT that have their own cloud computer and use your connected tools. As of 2026-11-24.

What is Grok Bot?

Its page describes Grok Bot as AI teammates that sign in to your tools and come back with finished work. It works in Slack, Microsoft Teams and its own desktop and iOS apps. As of 2026-11-24.

Is OpenAI Dots available in the EU?

For Plus and Pro, OpenAI says dots are not currently available in the European Economic Area (which includes the EU), the UK or Switzerland. Check OpenAI's help article for other plans. As of 2026-11-24.

How much does Grok Bot cost?

The page lists Premium at $30 a month, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200, with Enterprise through sales. Weekly usage is included, and extra usage is billed at token cost. As of 2026-11-24.

How much does OpenAI Dots cost?

We didn't find a dots price for individual plans on the OpenAI pages we read. For Enterprise, OpenAI describes flat rates mapped to the type of work, with no fee through 2027-01-13 and billing from 2027-01-14. As of 2026-11-24.

Which is better, OpenAI Dots or Grok Bot?

It depends on where your team works. Dots fits teams already in ChatGPT in a supported region. Grok Bot fits teams in Slack or Microsoft Teams who want listed prices.

Is there an alternative to both?

Yes. Orchy is a hosted AI workspace where agents work in your own apps and ask before they send, post, pay, change a calendar, delete or share. See Grok Bot alternatives.
