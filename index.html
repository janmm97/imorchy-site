FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Orchy?

Orchy is a hosted AI workspace with a team of AI agents. You tell it a task in plain words, the right agent does it in your connected apps, and it asks you before anything goes out.

Who is Orchy for?

Teams that want help with day-to-day work in Marketing, Operations, IT, Finance and Sales, and people who work for several clients. Each client gets a separate workspace.

Is Orchy hosted?

Yes. Orchy runs at OVHcloud in Canada, with encrypted backups in Eastern North America and one isolated container per customer. There is nothing to install.

Which AI does Orchy use?

AI usage comes with your plan, so there is no AI key to set up. You choose from a curated list of leading AI models.

What if an agent gets something wrong?

By default Orchy asks before it sends, posts, pays, changes a calendar, deletes or shares. You see the draft first and can approve it or turn it down.

Which apps does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, Google Sheets, GitHub and Stripe, and many more apps through One. You connect the ones you use.

Can I build my own agent?

Yes. Describe the job in plain words and the Agent Builder sets up the agent, with its apps and the actions it must ask about first.

How is Orchy different from an AI chatbot?

A chat window answers you. Orchy does the task in your apps and waits for your approval before anything goes out.

How do I get started?

Pick a plan and click "Get started" to sign up. Solo is $19, Team $59 and Agency $129 a month.
