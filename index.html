Common questions about Orchy

What is Orchy AI?

Orchy AI is a personal AI assistant for people who want help with their work, not another tool to learn. You ask in plain words, a dedicated AI helper does the job in the apps you already use, such as your inbox or calendar, and nothing goes out until you approve it.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You don't need any coding or technical skills to use Orchy. You type what you want in plain words, the way you'd message a person, and Orchy's helpers do the work. When something is ready to go out, you check it and tap Approve.

What can a personal AI assistant like Orchy do for me?

Orchy can sort your inbox and draft replies, prepare you for meetings, follow up with people you met, turn one post into a week of social content, answer customer emails using your policy and chase overdue invoices politely. Each job has its own helper, and you approve anything before it goes out.

What if Orchy gets something wrong?

Nothing goes out until you approve it, so you can fix or reject a draft before anyone sees it. Orchy asks before it sends, posts or deletes anything, and every action is kept on record, so you can always see what was done, when and in which app.

Which apps does Orchy work with?

Orchy works with everyday apps such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion and Stripe, with more to come. You connect the accounts you want Orchy to use, and its helpers work inside those apps for you, so you don't have to switch tools or copy and paste between them.

How is Orchy different from a chatbot like ChatGPT?

Orchy is built to finish tasks in your own apps, not just talk about them. A dedicated helper does each job where the work happens, keeps each account separate, and waits for your approval before anything is sent, posted or deleted, so you don't copy answers out of a chat window.

Where do I use Orchy?

You can open Orchy in your web browser at app.imorchy.dev, where it runs on Orchy's servers, or use the desktop app, which runs on your own computer. Either way, you tell Orchy what you need by typing, or by voice if you prefer, and approve the results.

Is my data safe with Orchy?

Orchy keeps each workspace separate, with its own connected accounts, so one client's or project's data doesn't mix with another's. Orchy asks before it sends, posts or deletes anything, and every action is kept on record, so you can see exactly what happened and when.
