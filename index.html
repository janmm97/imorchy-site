FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Orchy?

Orchy is an AI workspace for people who work for several clients. You tell it a task in plain words, the right agent does it in that client's own apps, and it asks you before anything goes out.

Who is Orchy for?

Virtual assistants, freelancers and small agencies who do work for more than one client. Each client gets a separate workspace.

How does Orchy keep my clients apart?

Each client has a workspace with its own apps, agents and files, and a task runs in one workspace. Orchy also runs in its own isolated container, one per customer.

What if an agent gets something wrong?

By default Orchy asks before it sends, posts, pays, changes a calendar or deletes. You see the draft first and can approve it or turn it down.

Which apps does it work with?

Through One, Orchy can connect to 1,000+ apps. You connect the apps each client uses to that client's workspace.

Can I build my own agent?

Yes. Describe the job in plain words and the Agent Builder sets up the agent, with its apps and the actions it must ask about first.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You type the task in plain words and approve the result. Technical settings sit behind a Details link.

Do I need my own AI key?

Yes. You bring a key from OpenRouter, Anthropic or OpenAI, and Orchy runs on your account with that provider.

Where is my data hosted?

Orchy is hosted at OVHcloud in Canada, with encrypted backups in Eastern North America and one isolated container per customer. The Security page has the details.

How is Orchy different from an AI chatbot?

A chat window answers you. Orchy does the task in the client's own apps, keeps each client in a separate workspace, and waits for your approval before anything goes out.
