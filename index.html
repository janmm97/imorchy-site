Questions, answered

What is Orchy AI?

Orchy is a hosted AI workspace. AI agents do tasks in your apps, like Gmail, Slack and Notion, and ask for your approval before anything is sent, posted, paid or deleted.

Is Orchy an AI virtual assistant?

Yes, and more than one. Orchy gives you a team of AI agents, each with a job, working in your apps. Every agent asks before it acts.

Do I need to install Orchy?

No. Orchy is hosted. Sign in at app.imorchy.dev and connect your apps.

How much does Orchy cost?

From $9 a month (Starter). Solo is $19, Pro $39 and Business $99 a month, with AI credits included. Yearly Solo, Pro and Business plans get 2 months free.
