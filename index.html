What is Orchy AI?

What is Orchy AI?

Orchy AI is a command center that brings your AI agents, apps and workspaces together on your own computer. You tell Orchy what you need, by typing or with optional voice. Orchy routes the task to the right workspace, agent and model, and you keep the final say on anything that goes out.

Where does Orchy AI run?

Orchy AI runs on your own computer as a local dashboard. You can open it from browsers you have paired with it. Orchy sends nothing to its authors. Your agents, workspaces and instructions live on your machine, and each workspace uses its own accounts for its own work.

Does Orchy AI act without asking?

Routine work moves along on its own, but Orchy AI asks before it sends, posts, spends, deletes, shares or changes your calendar. Those actions wait for your OK, and nothing goes out without it. You let go of the busywork and keep control of every decision that reaches the outside world.

Can I keep clients separate in Orchy AI?

Yes. In Orchy AI, each client gets its own workspace, logins, agents and instructions, so work for one client never runs with another client's account. Accounts are checked before every run. That makes Orchy AI a fit for virtual assistants and small AI agencies who handle several clients from one computer.

Which AI agents and apps does Orchy AI work with?

Orchy AI works with 7 harnesses: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider and a custom CLI. You choose what works for each workspace. Apps connect through One, which reaches 1000+ apps, including Gmail, Slack, Notion and Stripe, so your agents can work inside the tools you already use.

How do I get access to Orchy AI?

Orchy AI is in early access. Join the early-access list through the signup form on imorchy.dev and book 30 minutes with the team. On that call the team gets you access, connects your tools through One, and maps out the first agent worth building for your workspace. Setup happens on the call.
