What an AI agent does in sales

Sales has a lot of work before and after the conversation: finding the right people, writing the first message, keeping the list tidy and following up. An agent takes that preparation. When the next step is to send a message, it stops and asks you.

02

Lead research

  • Finds public information about a company and the person you plan to contact
  • Summarises it in a sheet or a Notion page, with the sources
  • Lists the leads that match your description

You check the research before you use it.

03

Outreach drafts

The agent drafts an email for each lead from your notes, in your tone. You edit and approve. Each message is sent only after you approve it.

04

CRM and sheet updates

The agent adds new leads to your pipeline sheet, notes each one's status and updates the next step. Deleting a row or sharing the sheet asks first.

05

Follow-up reminders

The agent drafts the follow-up when a thread goes quiet and reminds you which leads to chase. Nothing goes out until you approve.

Questions

FAQ

What is an AI sales agent?

An AI sales agent is an AI agent that does the preparation around selling in your own apps: lead research, outreach drafts, sheet updates and follow-up reminders. In Orchy it asks before it sends.

Can an AI agent send cold emails for me?

It can draft them. Each email is sent only after you approve it. You stay responsible for who you contact and for following the email rules that apply to you.

Can an AI agent update my CRM?

It can update the Google Sheet or Notion page you use as your CRM. Deleting or sharing asks first. For other CRMs, the agent can connect through One where the app is supported.

Will an AI agent replace my sales team?

Orchy's agents take on research, drafts and tracking. People talk to customers and decide what to send.
