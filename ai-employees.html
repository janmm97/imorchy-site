Questions, answered

What is an AI employee?

An AI agent with a clear role, like operations or marketing, that does tasks in your apps. In Orchy, every AI employee asks before it sends, posts, pays or deletes.

What is an AI teammate?

Another name for an AI employee: an agent that works with you on real tasks in your tools. Not a person.

Can AI employees replace staff?

They take routine work: sorting, research and drafts. People still decide. Nothing goes out without your approval.

How do AI employees get into my apps?

You connect apps through One, Orchy's app layer with 1,100+ apps. You choose what each agent may use.
