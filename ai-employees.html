What "AI employee" means

An AI employee is an AI agent with a job. It is software, not a person. You give it a role, such as answering the inbox, keeping the content calendar or triaging GitHub issues. It works in the apps you connect and reports back with drafts and results.

Some people say AI teammate, AI coworker or AI worker. They mean the same thing: an agent that takes on part of a team's work.

What they do alone, and what needs your OK

On their own: reading, research, drafting and taking notes. Ask first by default: sending or posting, paying, changing a calendar, deleting and sharing.

You see the request, then approve or deny it. You can choose "Always allow" for one action, for one agent or for a whole workspace. Gmail forwarding and filter changes always ask.

Questions

FAQ

What is an AI employee?

An AI employee is an AI agent with a defined job. It does that kind of work in your connected apps, such as drafting replies or triaging GitHub issues, and asks before it sends, posts, pays, changes a calendar, deletes or shares.

What is an AI teammate?

An AI teammate is another name for an AI employee: an agent that takes on part of a team's work. In Orchy, you build or pick one by describing its job in plain words.

Can AI employees replace staff?

Orchy's agents take on tasks: research, drafts, tracking and follow-ups. People decide what goes out and what to do next. We don't position Orchy as a replacement for people.

How do AI employees get access to my apps?

You connect apps through One, and you give each agent read or full access to the apps it needs. If an agent reaches for an app outside its list, Orchy asks you first.

What happens before an AI employee sends an email?

It drafts the email on its own, then stops and shows you what it wants to send. You approve or deny. Nothing is sent until you approve, unless you chose "Always allow" for sending.
