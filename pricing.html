Questions, answered

How much does an AI agent cost?

With Orchy, $9 to $99 a month, AI credits included. Starter is $9 and Solo $19. Pro ($39) and Business ($99) are coming soon.

What is an Orchy?

One hosted AI workspace with its own agents. Starter and Solo include 1, Pro 2, Business 5.

What happens when I run out of AI credits?

You get a warning at 80% of your monthly credits. At 100%, AI work pauses until your next month. Unused credits roll over to next month while your plan is active.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment, if no AI credits have been used.

Is there a yearly discount?

Yes. Solo, Pro and Business paid yearly get 2 months free. Starter is monthly only.
