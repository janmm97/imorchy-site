01

What's an Orchy?

An Orchy is one hosted copy of Orchy that is yours. Inside it you keep a separate workspace for each client, with their own apps, agents and files, and you give it tasks in plain words. A plan with more Orchys gives you more of these copies.

Client workspaces
02

What does AI credit buy?

AI credit pays for the AI work your agents do: reading, drafting, answering, working out the next step. Each time an agent uses AI, a small amount comes out of your credit. A short reply costs less than a long report. Credit comes with your plan every month.

We can't yet tell you how many tasks 100 credits cover. We're still measuring what real tasks cost, and it depends on the task. So we won't give you a number we can't back up. Orchy shows your usage as you go, warns you at 80%, and pauses AI work at 100% until next month.

Questions

FAQ

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in one case. If you ask within 14 days of your first payment and you haven't used any AI credits, you get a full refund. Renewals aren't refundable. The first time you start using AI, Orchy asks you to confirm, because using AI ends the refund.

What happens when I use all my AI credit?

At 80% Orchy warns you. At 100%, AI work pauses until next month, when your credit starts again. There's no top-up, and unused credit doesn't roll over.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and you keep access to the end of the period you've paid for. Renewals aren't refundable, so cancel before your renewal date if you don't want the next one.

Can I change plan?

Yes. An upgrade takes effect straight away. A downgrade takes effect on your next billing date.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes, on Solo, Pro and Business. A year costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free. Starter is monthly only.

What's an Orchy?

One hosted copy of Orchy that's yours, with a separate workspace for each client. Starter and Solo include 1, Pro 2, and Business 5.

Is my client data kept separate?

Each client has their own workspace with its own apps, agents and files, and a task runs in one workspace only.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. The AI credit in your plan covers the AI Orchy uses.
