Questions, answered

What can an AI agent do for my business?

Routine, checkable work: sort email, draft replies, update sheets, build reports, prep invoices, research leads. In Orchy, anything that goes out waits for your approval.

How are small businesses using AI agents?

Mostly admin: inbox triage, scheduling, follow-ups, reports and first drafts, with a person approving what's sent.

What is the best AI agent for business?

The one that works in your apps, asks before acting, and keeps client data apart. Compare tools on those three points.

Do AI agents act without asking?

Orchy's don't. Reading and drafting run alone. Sending, posting, paying, calendar changes, deleting and sharing wait for you.
