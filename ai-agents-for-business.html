Questions

FAQ

What is an AI agent for business?

An AI agent for business is software that does a task for you in your own apps, instead of only answering in a chat window. You describe the task in plain words. The agent reads, researches and drafts, then asks you before it sends, posts, pays, changes a calendar, deletes or shares.

What can an AI agent do for my business?

It can take on repeat work: research, drafts, tracking and follow-ups. In Orchy that includes inbox triage and reply drafts, post and newsletter drafts, GitHub issue triage, invoice drafts and lead research. The agent prepares the work and you approve what goes out.

How are small businesses using AI agents?

A common first job is the work that repeats every week: sorting the inbox, drafting replies and posts, updating a tracker or a sheet, and writing the follow-up. Start with one job, check the drafts, then add more. Approvals keep you in control while you decide how much to hand over.

How much does an AI agent cost?

Prices vary by product, and some vendors only publish "contact sales". With Orchy, AI usage comes with your plan, so there is no AI key to buy or set up. See Orchy's pricing.

Do AI agents act without asking?

By default Orchy's agents ask before they send, post, pay, change a calendar, delete or share. Reading, research and drafting run on their own. You can choose "Always allow" for an action, but that is your choice.

Is my business data safe with AI agents?

Here is what Orchy does: it is hosted at OVHcloud, each customer runs in one isolated container, and each client gets a separate workspace. Agents get only the apps you connect to them, at read or full access. The Security page has the details.

What is the best AI agent for business?

It depends on where your team works. Ask five things: where does the agent work (your apps, a chat window or a desktop), what does it ask before it acts, where is your data hosted, can you build agents for your own jobs, and is the price published.
