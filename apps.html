Questions, answered

Which apps does Orchy work with?

1,100+ apps through One, including Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub and Stripe.

Can an AI agent work in Gmail and Slack?

Yes. Connect them and agents read, sort and draft there. Sending waits for your approval.

Do I need a separate One account?

No. One is part of Orchy, so you connect apps from inside your workspace.

Can I limit which apps an agent uses?

Yes. You choose each agent's apps and access, per client workspace.
