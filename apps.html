Connect each client's apps to that client

Search the app list in Connections and connect the apps a client uses. Connections belong to a workspace, so one client's Gmail stays with that client.

03

Choose what each agent may use

You give each agent its apps at read or full access. If it reaches for another app in the workspace, Orchy asks you first. When the Agent Builder creates an agent, it picks the apps and you confirm them.

04

Checked on every call

Orchy checks every app call against the workspace's connections and the agent's access, and read-only access can only read. By default, sending, money, calendar changes, deleting and sharing ask you first.

Some changes always ask. A change to Gmail forwarding or a Gmail filter asks every time, and so does any change in GitHub on hosted Orchy unless you chose "always allow" for that action.

Questions

FAQ

Which apps does Orchy work with?

Through One, Orchy can connect to 1,000+ apps. You connect the apps each client uses to that client's workspace.

Can an agent use an app I haven't connected?

No. Orchy checks every app call against the workspace's connections and the agent's access.

Do I connect every client's accounts separately?

Yes. Each workspace has its own connections, so a client's accounts stay with that client's workspace.

Will an agent send email without asking me?

By default Orchy asks before it sends. You approve or turn it down, and a change to Gmail forwarding always asks.

Who runs One?

One Systems, Inc., the company behind Orchy. In hosted Orchy, One is part of our service.
