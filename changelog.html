Stricter browser security settings

2026-11-02

  • Browsers are told to use only HTTPS for the site, not to show it inside other sites' frames, and to load scripts only from the places the site needs. Pages other than the homepage run no scripts.

Answer pages, an FAQ and a faster site on phones

2026-10-30

  • The homepage has an FAQ, an "Orchy AI is in early access." badge, a "Join the early-access list" button and a "Book 30 minutes" link. Addresses that start with http:// or www. now go straight to https://imorchy.dev.
  • Four answer pages: Approvals, Client workspaces, Claude Code orchestrator and Local command center. Each has its own FAQ and works without JavaScript, and the homepage links to them from its Learn menu.
  • The site is faster on phones: lighter images and fonts, and the 3D squad waits until the page has loaded. On phones, with Save-Data on or with reduced motion, a still picture shows instead. In a local Lighthouse mobile test, the largest contentful paint (LCP) went from 3.08 s to 2.48 s.
  • The homepage footer has "Back to the Top", the answer pages' footers have "Back to Home", and the answer pages show the homepage logo.
  • The FAQs open and close on click, work with the keyboard and without JavaScript, and are centred.
  • Every page uses the same Orchy icon in the browser tab.

Questions, answered

How often is Orchy updated?

Orchy ships updates regularly. Each release is listed here with its date.

Do I need to update anything?

No. Orchy is hosted, so updates reach your workspace automatically.

Where can I report a problem?

Email support@imorchy.dev. We aim to reply within 5 business days.

Hand off the work. Approve what goes out.

Tell Orchy a task once, in plain words. It does the work in your apps and waits for your yes.
