Grok Bot alternatives

The main Grok Bot alternatives are OpenAI Dots, Claude Cowork, Lindy and Orchy. Pick by where your team works and how approvals are handled.

Try OrchyCompare Dots

Grok Bot at a glance

Its page describes Grok Bot as "AI teammates you can give real work to". It works in Slack, Microsoft Teams and desktop and iOS apps, and bots can attend Google Meet, Zoom or Teams calls. You can show a bot a workflow once, and there is a Marketplace. Plans listed: Premium $30 a month, Super $100, Ultra $200 and Enterprise through sales, with weekly usage included and extra usage billed at token cost. Source: x.ai/bot, as of 2026-11-24.

Why teams look for an alternative

  • They don't work in Slack or Microsoft Teams.
  • They want agents inside the apps they already use, such as Gmail, Notion or Google Sheets.
  • They want to know where their data is hosted. The Grok Bot page doesn't state a region.
  • They want a different price shape than the listed tiers.

What to look for

  • Where it works: your apps, a chat window or a desktop.
  • What it asks before it acts: sending, paying, deleting, sharing.
  • Where your data is hosted.
  • Whether you can build agents for your own jobs.
  • How it is priced: listed, per seat, per usage or on request.

The alternatives

OpenAI Dots

Agents inside ChatGPT, with Custom Rules that allow, require approval for or block actions. You can create your own dot. For Plus and Pro, not currently available in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland. No dots price for individual plans on the pages we read. Sources: chatgpt.com/features/dots/ and help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530. Best for teams already working in ChatGPT in a supported region.

Claude Cowork

Takes real actions on your files and in connected tools, on web, desktop and mobile. You choose the folders and tools, deleting anything needs your approval, and with permission settings on it shows its plan and waits for your approval before anything significant. Plugins and skills extend it. Listed prices: Pro $20 a month ($17 on annual billing), Max $100, Ultra $200, Team $20 per seat per month. Source: claude.com/product/cowork. Best for people who want an assistant that works on their own files and tools.

Lindy

Works in Slack and iMessage, with 40+ skills plus the option to create your own, and lists 3,000+ integrations. Anything with outside impact, such as sending an email, waits for approval. A free start with $50 in credits that last 7 days; Team at $29.99 per active user per month for 3,000 credits. Source: lindy.ai/pricing. Best for people who want a low-cost way to start.

Orchy

A hosted AI workspace where a team of AI agents works in your connected apps (Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, Google Sheets, GitHub and Stripe, and more through One). Asks first by default before it send, post, pay, change a calendar, delete or share. Build new agents in plain words with the Agent Builder. AI usage comes with your plan, with no AI key to set up. Hosted at OVHcloud, with a separate workspace for each client. Best for teams that want agents in their own apps, by function, with approvals by default.

Best pick by use case

  • Your team lives in Slack or Teams: Grok Bot or Lindy.
  • Your team lives in ChatGPT: OpenAI Dots.
  • You want an assistant on your own files and tools: Claude Cowork.

Questions, answered

What is comparable to a Grok Bot?

OpenAI Dots, Claude Cowork, Lindy and Orchy all run AI agents that do real tasks. They differ in where they work and how they ask for approval.

How much does Grok Bot cost?

As of 2026-11-24, Grok Bot's page lists Premium at $30, Super at $100 and Ultra at $200 a month, with Enterprise through sales.

Is there a Grok Bot alternative that asks before it acts?

Yes. Orchy asks by default before it sends, posts, pays, changes a calendar, deletes or shares. OpenAI Dots can require approval through Custom Rules, and Claude Cowork asks before deleting files (as of 2026-11-24).
