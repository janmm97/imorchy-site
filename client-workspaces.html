Separate AI agents and logins for every client

Orchy AI gives each client its own workspace, logins, agents and instructions, all on your own computer. Accounts are checked before every run, so one client's work never runs with another client's login.

Orchy AI is built for people who juggle several clients: virtual assistants, small AI agencies and business owners with more than one project. Different worlds. No crossed wires.

How it works

  1. One workspace per client. Each client gets its own workspace, with its own logins, agents and instructions.
  2. Choose the tools per workspace. Pick the brain that works for each workspace (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider or a custom CLI) and connect that client's apps through One.
  3. Tell Orchy what you need. Orchy finds the right workspace, teammate and model, and routes the task there.
  4. Accounts are checked before every run. Your agents work inside the right workspace, with the right account.
  5. You keep the final say. Sending, posting, spending, deleting, sharing and calendar changes wait for your OK, in every workspace.

Example

Sample, as shown on imorchy.dev. Your dashboard lists three workspaces: "Your studio" with 3 agents, "Client project" with 2 agents and "Personal projects" with 4 agents. Each one runs on its own account. When you ask for an inbox agent in the client's workspace, it uses that client's login and that client's instructions, and its replies still wait for your OK before they go out.
