Separate AI agents and logins for every client

This page describes the desktop app. Hosted Orchy is at app.imorchy.dev.

Orchy AI gives each client its own workspace, logins, agents and instructions, all on your own computer.

Orchy AI is built for people who juggle several clients: virtual assistants, small AI agencies and business owners with more than one project. Different worlds. No crossed wires.

How it works

  1. One workspace per client. Each client gets its own workspace, with its own logins, agents and instructions.
  2. Choose the tools per workspace. Pick the brain that works for each workspace (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider or a custom CLI) and connect that client's apps through One.
  3. Tell Orchy what you need. Orchy finds the right workspace, teammate and model, and routes the task there.
  4. You keep the final say. Sending, posting and deleting wait for your OK, in every workspace.

Example

Sample, as shown on imorchy.dev. Your dashboard lists three workspaces: "Your studio" with 3 agents, "Client project" with 2 agents and "Personal projects" with 4 agents. Each one runs on its own account. When you ask for an inbox agent in the client's workspace, it uses that client's login and that client's instructions, and its replies still wait for your OK before they go out.

FAQ

Can I keep each client's logins separate in Orchy AI?

Yes. In Orchy AI, each client gets its own workspace, logins, agents and instructions. Different worlds, no crossed wires, all managed from one local dashboard on your computer.

Is Orchy AI a fit for a VA with several clients?

Orchy AI is built for virtual assistants who handle several clients. Each client has its own workspace, so its logins, agents and instructions stay apart. Routine work moves along, and anything that sends, posts or deletes waits for your OK first.

Can a small AI agency run client agents in Orchy AI?

Yes. A small AI agency can give every client a workspace in Orchy AI, with its own agents, logins and instructions. You choose the brain that works for each workspace and connect the client's apps through One, which reaches 1000+ apps.

Can different clients use different AI agents?

Yes. Orchy AI works with 7 harnesses: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider and a custom CLI. You choose what works for each workspace, so one client's workspace can use a different brain from another's. Each keeps its own logins and instructions.

How do I set up client workspaces in Orchy AI?

Orchy AI is in invite-only early access. Select Get Started to open app.imorchy.dev, then sign in with Google or an email link to join the waiting list. We approve each sign-up by hand and let people in in small groups. During early access we run at most 20 customer Orchys at a time.
