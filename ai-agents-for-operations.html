What an AI agent does in operations

Operations is a long list of small jobs that keep a team moving. An operations agent takes the preparation: it reads, sorts, drafts and files. When the next step is to send a reply, book a meeting or delete something, it stops and asks you.

Questions

FAQ

What is an AI agent for operations?

It is an AI agent that does operations tasks in your own apps: inbox triage, reply drafts, scheduling, trackers and SOPs. In Orchy it asks before it sends, books or deletes.

Can an AI agent manage my inbox?

It can read the inbox, sort it and draft replies. Sending waits for your approval. Orchy's agents don't change Gmail forwarding or filters without asking.

Can an AI agent schedule meetings?

It can propose times from your Google Calendar and draft the invite. The meeting is booked once you approve it.

Can it write our SOPs?

Yes. Describe the process or share the notes and emails, and the agent drafts the SOP in Notion or a doc. You review and edit it before it is shared.
