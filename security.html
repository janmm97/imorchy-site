01

Hosted in Canada

Orchy runs on a server at OVHcloud in Canada. Encrypted backups are stored in Eastern North America.

02

One isolated container per customer

Your Orchy runs in its own container. Requests pass an authentication check before they reach it, and your agents run as a separate user with no system privileges. A customer's container can't reach the server itself or private networks. Web traffic reaches our server only through a Cloudflare Tunnel.

03

Your own AI key

Bring a key from OpenRouter, Anthropic or OpenAI. Orchy stores your key sealed with AES-256-GCM and uses it only to make your agents' calls. Your agents send prompts and files to the provider you chose, under your terms with it. On hosted Orchy, the text of each request is also sent to TypeSafe, our routing provider, so it can pick how to handle the task.

04

Approvals before anything goes out

By default, Orchy asks before it sends or posts, spends money, changes a calendar, deletes or shares.

How approvals work
  • Files, published copies, emailWithin 30 days
  • Final encrypted backup14 days after it was made
  • Run log, no message bodies90 days
05

Delete when you want

Click "Delete my Orchy" and your files, published copies and email are deleted within 30 days. Your final encrypted backup is removed 14 days after it was made, as every backup is. OVHcloud's server backups are replaced within 7 days. The run log for connected apps keeps no message bodies and is kept for 90 days.

  • Training on your hosted contentNever
  • SOC 2Not held
  • ISO 27001Not held
06

Who handles your data

We never use your hosted content to train AI models. One Connect, which connects your apps, is our own service. The companies that help us run hosted Orchy are listed on the Data Privacy page, with our data processing agreement. We hold no security certifications, such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001. This page describes what our software and server do, not an audit.

Questions

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Orchy is hosted in Canada, on a server at OVHcloud, with a daily OVHcloud server backup plus our own encrypted backups in Eastern North America. Each customer has an isolated container.

Who can see my clients' data?

We don't look at it except as needed to keep the service running, fix problems you ask us to fix, or meet the law.

Does Orchy train AI models on my data?

No. We never use your hosted content to train or improve AI models.

Do I need my own AI key?

Yes. You bring a key from OpenRouter, Anthropic or OpenAI. Orchy stores it sealed.

How do I delete my data?

Use "Delete my Orchy" in your account. Your files, published copies and email are deleted within 30 days, and the final encrypted backup is removed 14 days after it was made. OVHcloud's server backups are replaced within 7 days.

Do you hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

No. We hold no security certifications, and we list only the measures that are in place.

How do I report a security problem?

Email support@imorchy.dev. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
