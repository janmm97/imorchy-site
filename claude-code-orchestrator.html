Run Claude Code, Codex and Cursor as one team

Orchy AI is a local dashboard that runs Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other coding agents as one team on your own computer. You tell Orchy AI what you need, and it routes the task to the right workspace, teammate and model.

Orchy AI works with 7 harnesses: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider and a custom CLI. Your tools. Now a team.

How it works

  1. Choose a brain for each workspace. Pick the harness that works for each workspace: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider or a custom CLI.
  2. Connect your apps. Your agents reach Gmail, Slack, Notion, Stripe and 1000+ apps through One.
  3. Ask once. Type it out, or say it out loud with optional voice. One conversation starts the work.
  4. Orchy routes the task. Orchy finds the right workspace, teammate and model, and your agents work with the right account.
  5. You keep the final say. Sending, posting, spending, deleting, sharing and calendar changes wait for your OK.

Example

Illustration. You run three workspaces: one uses Claude Code, one uses Codex and one uses Cursor. From the one dashboard you ask for a task in the second workspace. Orchy routes it there, and that workspace's agent does the work with that workspace's account. If the task ends in a send or a post, Orchy asks you first.

FAQ

Can I manage Claude Code and Codex from one dashboard?

Yes. Orchy AI runs Claude Code, Codex and five more harnesses (Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider and a custom CLI) from one local dashboard on your computer. You choose the brain for each workspace, tell Orchy what you need, and Orchy routes the task to the right workspace, teammate and model.

Which coding agents does Orchy AI support?

Orchy AI supports 7 harnesses: Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Cursor, Antigravity, Aider and a custom CLI. You choose what works for each workspace. Your agents can also reach Gmail, Slack, Notion, Stripe and 1000+ apps through One, so they work inside the tools you already use.

How does Orchy AI decide which agent does a task?

You bring the idea, and Orchy AI finds the right workspace, teammate and model to make it happen. Orchy routes the task, and your agents work inside the right workspace, with the right account. Accounts are checked before every run, so work never lands in the wrong place.

Do my coding agents still ask before they act?

Yes. Whichever harness a workspace uses, Orchy AI asks before it sends, posts, spends, deletes, shares or changes your calendar. Routine work moves along on its own. Nothing goes out without your OK, so you can hand off the busywork and keep the controls.
