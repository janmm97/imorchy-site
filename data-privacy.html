Data Privacy: DPA and sub-processors

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Version 3.1

What changed in Version 3.1

  • OpenRouter is added as a sub-processor, because Orchy now runs your agents' AI on its own account.
  • Bring-your-own AI keys are no longer offered.
  • Payments run through Polar, a separate controller (second table).

This page is for businesses that use Orchy and need to know who handles their data and on what terms.

1. What hosted Orchy stores, and where

Hosted Orchy runs on our server in North America, so what you put in it is stored with us: tasks, files, logs, settings, your agents' memory and chat history, and records from the apps you connect. It sits in your own container on our server. The server has OVHcloud's daily automated backup, and our own encrypted backups are kept with Cloudflare in Eastern North America.

You are the controller and we are your processor, so our DPA applies (section 2). OpenRouter, which runs your agents' AI on Orchy's own account, is our sub-processor (section 3). One Connect is our own service, covered by the DPA directly (section 3).

2. Hosted Orchy (app.imorchy.dev): Orchy is your processor

When you use hosted Orchy, what you put in it is stored on our server in North America. You are the controller; we are your processor. We use it only to run your Orchy, and never to train or improve AI models. Our Data Processing Agreement is part of our Terms. It is based on the Common Paper DPA v1.1 and covers what GDPR Art. 28 requires: instructions only, confidentiality, security, sub-processor rules, help with data-subject requests and breaches, deletion or return, and audits.

For account, sign-in and billing data, Orchy is the controller; see the Privacy Policy.

Common Paper Data Processing Agreement (v1.1) is free to use under CC BY 4.0, https://commonpaper.com/standards/data-processing-agreement/. Adapted by One Systems, Inc.

4. Where data is and how long we keep it

Hosted data is on our server in North America; encrypted backups are in Eastern North America. Your files, published copies and email are deleted within 30 days of "Delete my Orchy" or of access being removed; your final encrypted backup is removed 14 days after it was made, as every backup is. OVHcloud's server backups are replaced within 7 days. Transfers outside the EU and UK rely on the safeguards in the DPA (section 9).
