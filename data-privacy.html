Data Privacy: DPA and sub-processors

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Version 2.0

This page is for businesses that use Orchy and need to know who handles their data and on what terms.

1. Desktop app: Orchy does not process your data

The desktop app runs on your own computer. Your data stays there and Orchy does not receive it. You are the controller, and Orchy is not your processor, so no DPA is needed for the app. When you connect outside services (your AI engine, a routing provider, ElevenLabs, Deepgram, Cloudflare), you deal with them directly under your own accounts and their terms. They are your vendors, not ours. One (withone.ai), which connects your apps, is run by our own company, One Systems, Inc.; with your own One account, One's terms and privacy policy apply.

2. Hosted Orchy (app.imorchy.dev): Orchy is your processor

When you use hosted Orchy, what you put in it is stored on our server in Canada. You are the controller; we are your processor. We use it only to run your Orchy, and never to train or improve AI models. Our Data Processing Agreement is part of our Terms. It is based on the Common Paper DPA v1.1 and covers what GDPR Art. 28 requires: instructions only, confidentiality, security, sub-processor rules, help with data-subject requests and breaches, deletion or return, and audits.

For account, sign-in and waiting-list data, Orchy is the controller; see the Privacy Policy.

Common Paper Data Processing Agreement (v1.1) is free to use under CC BY 4.0, https://commonpaper.com/standards/data-processing-agreement/. Adapted by One Systems, Inc.

Access calls: if we help you set up on a Google Meet call, we don't record it in Meet; an AI note-taker (Granola) takes notes and a transcript. We tell you at booking and at the start of the call, and turn notes off if you ask. We delete notes after 12 months or sooner on request. You share your own screen and paste your own key; we don't copy or keep your keys, emails or files.

4. Where data is and how long we keep it

Hosted data is on our server in Canada; encrypted backups are in Eastern North America. Your files, published copies and email are deleted within 30 days of "Delete my Orchy" or of access being removed; your final encrypted backup is removed 14 days after it was made, as every backup is. Transfers outside the EU and UK rely on the safeguards in the DPA (section 9).
